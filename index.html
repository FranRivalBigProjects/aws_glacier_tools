Glacier Tier Guide

S3 Glacier Instant Retrieval — ~$0.004/GB/month
For data accessed once per quarter with millisecond retrieval. Same retrieval performance as S3 Standard but 68% cheaper for storage. Minimum storage duration: 90 days. No retrieval charge for Standard retrievals up to 10% of stored data. Best for: medical images, media assets, backups accessed occasionally but urgently when needed.

S3 Glacier Flexible Retrieval — ~$0.0036/GB/month
For archival data accessed 1-2 times per year. Retrieval options: Expedited (1-5 min, $0.03/GB), Standard (3-5 hours, $0.01/GB), Bulk (5-12 hours, free). Minimum storage duration: 90 days. Early deletion fee applies if objects are removed before 90 days. Best for: disaster recovery, compliance archives, project backups.

S3 Glacier Deep Archive — ~$0.00099/GB/month
The lowest-cost AWS storage option — 75% cheaper than Glacier Flexible. Retrieval takes 12 hours (Standard) or up to 48 hours (Bulk). Minimum storage duration: 180 days. Early deletion fee is significant for frequently changed data. Best for: long-term regulatory compliance, data that must be retained but rarely accessed, replacing tape-based archival systems.

Early deletion penalty explained
If you store 100GB in Glacier Flexible for 30 days and delete it, you are billed for 60 remaining days at the Glacier Flexible rate: 100 GB × 60 days × ($0.0036 / 30 days) = $0.72. For Deep Archive the minimum is 180 days — deleting after 60 days means a 120-day penalty. The calculator above computes this automatically.

FAQ — Amazon Glacier Pricing
Amazon Glacier storage prices (US East, per GB/month): Glacier Instant Retrieval ~$0.004. Glacier Flexible Retrieval ~$0.0036. Glacier Deep Archive ~$0.00099. For comparison, S3 Standard is ~$0.023/GB/month — Glacier Deep Archive is about 23x cheaper for storage. Retrieval costs are separate and depend on tier and retrieval speed.
Glacier Flexible Retrieval: Expedited $0.03/GB (1-5 min), Standard $0.01/GB (3-5 hrs), Bulk free (5-12 hrs). Glacier Instant Retrieval: $0.03/GB (milliseconds). Glacier Deep Archive: Standard $0.02/GB (12 hrs), Bulk $0.0025/GB (48 hrs). First 10GB of retrieval per month is free. For large-scale retrieval, Bulk tier is free for Flexible and very cheap for Deep Archive.
Glacier Flexible Retrieval has a 90-day minimum. Glacier Deep Archive has a 180-day minimum. Early deletion is charged for remaining days: delete a 1GB Flexible object after 60 days = 30 days of penalty at $0.0036/30 = $0.0036 × (30/30) = $0.0036. For Deep Archive, deleting 100GB after 30 days = 150 days penalty = 100 × (150/30) × $0.00099 = $0.495. The calculator above shows this automatically.
Choose Glacier Flexible if you may need data within hours on short notice — retrieval takes 3-5 hours with Standard tier. Choose Deep Archive if data is accessed once a year or less and you can wait 12+ hours — it's 75% cheaper. If you need immediate access (seconds), use Glacier Instant Retrieval. If cost is the only factor and data is compliance-only, Deep Archive is the right choice.
